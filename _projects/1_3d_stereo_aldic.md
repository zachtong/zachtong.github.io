An advanced, robust, and user-friendly open-source stereo-DIC method that combines adaptive mesh refinement with Lagrangian tracking for high-accuracy 3D displacement measurement.

**Key Features:**

- Adaptive mesh refinement for complex geometries
- Augmented Lagrangian formulation for large deformations
- User-friendly GUI for stereo calibration and analysis
- Validated on biological tissue and metamaterial specimens

**Publication:** [3D Stereo Adaptive Mesh Augmented Lagrangian Digital Image Correlation](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11340-025-01225-7), _Experimental Mechanics_, 2025
