I build tools that **measure and validate how real hardware deforms and behaves** — combining **full-field optical metrology**, **3D and stereo vision**, **camera calibration**, and **machine learning**. My work turns difficult physical measurements into reliable, automated, software-driven workflows.

Technically, this centers on **Digital Image / Volume Correlation (DIC/DVC)** and dense image registration — recovering full-field displacement and strain under complex geometry, large deformation, and challenging real-world conditions — and on connecting **experiments, simulation, and data-driven models**.

I'm currently an **R&D intern at Tokyo Electron**, working on simulation-informed modeling for semiconductor equipment. I'm graduating in **2027** and interested in industry roles across **optical metrology, hardware characterization, camera and imaging systems, mechanical simulation and reliability, and ML for physical systems**.

**Focus areas**
- Full-field optical metrology — DIC/DVC, dense image registration
- 3D and stereo vision · camera calibration · 3D reconstruction
- Machine learning for measurement and physical systems
- Simulation-informed modeling and digital twins — FEA (Abaqus, COMSOL)
- Hardware deformation, thermal-mechanical, and reliability measurement
