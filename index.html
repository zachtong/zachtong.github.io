What I do

Measure. Model. Validate.

I work across the full mechanics loop—from loading a specimen to measuring its response, identifying its properties, and testing the model.

01

Measure

Observe material response under real loading with quantitative, full-field experiments.

  • DIC & DVC
  • High-speed imaging
  • Instrumented testing
02

Model

Represent deformation, stress, interfaces, and rate effects with physics-based models.

  • Solid & continuum mechanics
  • Finite element analysis
  • Thermo-mechanical systems
03

Identify & validate

Use measured fields to recover material properties and quantify whether a simulation predicts reality.

  • Inverse identification
  • Simulation–test correlation
  • Learning-accelerated models
